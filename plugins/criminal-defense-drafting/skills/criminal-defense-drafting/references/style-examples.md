# Style: do and do not

Neutral model formulations. Adapt to the forum's conventions. All case and rule references here are placeholders.

## Argument
**Do not:** "The investigator grossly violated the law and the evidence is obviously worthless."
**Do:** "The search was carried out outside the scope of the warrant (Vol. __, p. __). The items listed at lines 4 to 9 of the inventory are not described in the warrant. The defense submits that they should be excluded: [AUTHORITY: …]."

**Do not:** "The case has collapsed and the prosecution has no evidence."
**Do:** "The prosecution relies on a single witness. Her account of the date differs from the document at p. __. On that evidence the court cannot safely be satisfied of the element of [__]."

**Do not:** "Clearly, absolutely nothing supports the charge."
**Do:** "The material before the court does not establish the element of [__] to the required standard."

## Facts
**Do not:** "The police brutally detained the client."
**Do:** "The client was detained at 14:10 on [date] (Vol. __, p. __). He was not offered access to counsel until 19:40 (p. __)."

## Standard and burden
**Do:** "The burden of proof on this issue lies on the prosecution. The standard is [___] `[AUTHORITY: …]`."

## Relief sought
**Do not:** "I ask the court to do justice."
**Do:** "The defense asks the court to: (1) exclude the statement recorded at Vol. __, pp. __ to __; (2) alternatively, direct that an admissibility hearing be held before the statement is put to the jury; (3) such further relief as the court considers just."

## Respect for the court
**Do:** "With respect, the defense submits that the ruling at p. __ overlooked the document at Vol. __, p. __."
**Do not:** "The judge ignored the law."

## Probabilistic wording
**Do not:** "This proves innocence."
**Do:** "This is consistent with the defense account and raises a reasonable doubt as to [__]."

## Alternatives for key formulations
When a key formulation has two defensible versions, offer both after the main draft:
- *Version A (narrower, safer):* "…the statement should be excluded as obtained without a lawful caution."
- *Version B (broader):* "…the statement and all evidence derived from it should be excluded."
Note the risk of each: the broader ground may fail entirely if one limb is rejected.

## Formal elements
- Numbered paragraphs for the argument; numbered items in the relief.
- Consistent defined terms ("the Statement", "the Warrant").
- Record references in a uniform format.
- Placeholders in brackets, never in the middle of a sentence's grammar: `[date]`, `[AUTHORITY: …]`.

# Key principles and issues by scenario

This reference lists the issues the defense most often argues, grouped by scenario. It contains **no citations**: for each issue it names the type of authority to find, so the lawyer can supply the correct, current, binding authority for the forum. Never invent a citation. Mark every authority `[VERIFY]` until checked.

## Detention, bail, and conditions
- Presumption of liberty and the burden of justifying detention.
- Individualized risk assessment: flight, interference, reoffending, danger: each must rest on evidence, not on the gravity of the charge alone.
- Proportionality of conditions and the least restrictive alternative.
- Custody time limits and periodic review.
- Authorities to find: the bail statute and its leading appellate decisions; the review standard.

## Arrest, custody, and interviews
- Lawfulness of arrest and grounds; the time limits to bring before a court.
- Rights: to counsel, to silence, to an interpreter, to notification; recording of interviews.
- Consequences of breach: exclusion, adverse inference, abuse of process.
- Authorities to find: arrest and custody rules; the leading cases on advisements and voluntariness.

## Search, seizure, interception
- Warrant requirements: particularity, authorization, scope, execution, and the inventory.
- Seizures beyond the warrant; consent and its limits; digital searches and device access.
- Derivative evidence and its admissibility.
- Authorities to find: the search provisions; the leading exclusion decisions in the forum.

## Disclosure and unused material
- The prosecution's duty to disclose exculpatory and impeaching material, and the process for dispute.
- Lost or destroyed material and the remedies (adverse inference, exclusion, stay).
- Informant and surveillance material and the public-interest-immunity or equivalent claims.
- Authorities to find: the disclosure regime and the leading cases.

## Admissibility and evidence
- Hearsay and exceptions; confessions; bad character and similar-fact evidence; opinion and expert evidence; identification evidence; digital evidence and authentication.
- Fairness discretion and the balance of probative value against prejudice.
- Authorities to find: the evidence code or common-law rules; the expert-admissibility standard.

## Entrapment and undercover operations
- Who initiated; whether the authorities induced the offence; the basis for the operation and its authorization; the informant's role and rewards.
- Remedies: stay, exclusion, mitigation.
- Authorities to find: the entrapment doctrine in the forum.

## Trial
- Directed verdict or submission of no case to answer; jury directions and misdirection; joinder and severance; the right to a fair hearing.
- Authorities to find: the test for no case to answer; the pattern jury directions.

## Sentencing
- Sentencing principles and the guidelines; aggravating and mitigating factors; totality and proportionality; credit for time served and for pleas; alternatives to custody.
- Authorities to find: the guidelines and the leading appellate sentencing decisions.

## Appeal
- Grounds and the standard of review; preservation of error; fresh evidence; harmless error or the proviso; the risk of a retrial or increased sentence.
- Authorities to find: the appellate rules and the leading decisions on the relevant ground.

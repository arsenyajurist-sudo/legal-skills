# Catalogue of defense lines

Frameworks, not rules: test each against the real law of the forum and mark specifics `[VERIFY]`.

## Level 1. There should be no case at all
- **No offence in law:** the conduct does not satisfy the statute, or the statute does not apply (territory, time, status of the defendant).
- **Failure of a material element:** no mental state, no causation, no value above the threshold, wrong victim or property.
- **Identification and presence:** alibi, mistaken identity, unreliable identification procedures.
- **Justification and excuse:** self-defense, necessity, duress, consent, lawful authority, insanity or incapacity where available: with the burden rules of the forum.
- **Unlawful or abusive process:** abuse of process, entrapment, unlawful investigation so serious that the case should be stayed or dismissed.
- **Limitation or time bars** where the offence is subject to them.

## Level 2. Non-conviction dispositions
- Discontinuance, diversion, deferred prosecution, conditional discharge, mediation or restorative processes, civil settlement with compensation, caution or warning.
- Price: usually requires some accommodation (admission, compensation, conditions); check whether the disposition creates a record or consequences for immigration, professional licences, or employment.

## Level 3. Reclassification
- A lesser or alternative offence: lower mental state, lower value, lesser role.
- Removing an aggravating factor or a count so that the sentence range shrinks.
- Price: may require a concession on facts; check the effect on the appeal and on collateral consequences.

## Level 4. Working on the sentence
- Mitigation: the client's background, remorse, cooperation, compensation, health, family, delay, proportionality.
- Sentencing guidelines or ranges: prepare the arithmetic for each variant, and ask for a concrete figure.
- Alternatives to custody: suspended or community sentences, fines, treatment, house arrest, early release schemes.
- Credit for time served and for early pleas.

## Level 5. Negotiated or accelerated routes: count, do not simply agree
- **Plea bargaining:** charge negotiation and sentence agreements. Calculate the real difference between a plea and trial, including the trial penalty and the collateral consequences. The decision to plead belongs to the client.
- **Cooperation agreements:** the price is high (testimony, risk to the client, loss of defenses); the benefit must be written and enforceable.
- **Fast-track procedures:** consider the waiver of rights they usually involve.

## Level 6. Procedural lines that do not decide the case but matter
- Challenges to the court, the judge, the panel, or the jury; change of venue.
- Disclosure applications and abuse-of-process claims for lost or destroyed material.
- Severance or joinder; admissibility of co-defendant statements.
- Preserving every point for appeal.

## Mutually exclusive pairs
- "I was not there" vs "I acted in self-defense".
- "I did not know" vs "it was authorized and lawful".
- "Deny the act" vs "full plea in mitigation".
- A line that attacks the complainant's honesty vs a settlement strategy that depends on their goodwill.
Name the pair, choose the main line, and drop the fallback that would kill it.

## Choosing between lines: quick rules
- Prefer the line that needs the least from evidence you do not hold.
- If the prosecution's case rests on one witness or one piece of evidence, the line is to remove that pillar.
- If two lines are strong, pick the one whose failure costs less.
- Always price the line you are not choosing.

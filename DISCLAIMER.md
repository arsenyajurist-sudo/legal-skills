# Disclaimer

These skills and plugins are tools for **licensed lawyers and people working under their supervision**. They are not legal advice, do not create a lawyer-client relationship, and do not replace professional judgment.

- **Verify everything.** AI can invent case citations, statutes, and quotations. Treat every authority an AI proposes as a hypothesis until you have checked it in the official source.
- **Responsibility stays with you.** The lawyer who signs or files a document is responsible for its content.
- **Confidentiality.** Do not upload client secrets or personal data to any AI service unless your professional rules, your client agreement, and the provider's terms allow it. Anonymize first; Red Team Brief has a built-in anonymization step.
- **Jurisdiction.** Unless a skill says otherwise, the skills are jurisdiction-neutral frameworks. Rules of procedure, deadlines, and ethics differ by country and court.
